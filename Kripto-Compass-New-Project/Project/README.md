## Usage notes

- The brand, hero, and service-icon files have genuine transparent alpha backgrounds.
- The portfolio images are original concept art prepared for the proposed Krypto Compass website. They are not screenshots of shipped products, gameplay, financial results, or live trading software.
- Third-party company logos shown as placeholder social proof in the concept mockup are intentionally not included. Only display partner/customer marks after authorization and verification.
- Keep the supplied PNGs as master files. Export WebP or AVIF derivatives from these masters when optimizing a production website.

## Color profile and preferred formats

- Treat the supplied PNG masters as sRGB web assets. Preserve the sRGB profile when creating derivatives and verify color appearance after conversion.
- Keep PNG as the archival/master format, especially for artwork requiring transparency.
- Prefer AVIF for the smallest production delivery size when browser and quality testing pass; provide WebP as a broadly supported optimized fallback and PNG as the lossless or transparency fallback.
- Do not upscale the masters. Generate responsive derivatives at the actual display sizes and retain alpha transparency where applicable.

## Accessibility

- Give informative images concise alt text that communicates their purpose in context rather than describing every decorative detail.
- Use empty alt text (`alt=""`) for purely decorative artwork so assistive technology can ignore it.
- Do not place essential instructions or information only inside an image. Repeat meaningful text in accessible HTML.
- Avoid redundant wording such as “image of” or “icon of,” and ensure linked images describe the link destination or action.
